Day 1
Monday July 6
Londorossi Gate → Mti Mkubwa Camp
⛺ Mti Mkubwa Camp · 8,694' / 2,650m
4 mi · 3–4 hrs · Gentle ascent through montane rainforest. Colobus monkeys overhead. Tents pre-set on arrival by porters.
Jul 7
Day 2
Tuesday July 7
Mti Mkubwa → Shira Camp
⛺ Shira Camp · 11,500' / 3,505m
5 mi · 5–7 hrs · Leave rainforest. Enter heath and moorland. First views of Kilimanjaro's snow cap. Drink water constantly — altitude effects begin.
Jul 8
Day 3
Wednesday July 8
Shira Camp → Moir Camp
⛺ Moir Camp · 13,650' / 4,155m
6 mi · 6–8 hrs · Trek east across Shira Plateau. Optional detour to Shira Cone volcano. Camp in sheltered valley on dormant lava flow.
Jul 9
Day 4
Thursday July 9
Moir → Lava Tower (acclimatization) → Barranco
⛺ Barranco Camp · 12,950' · High point 15,190'
7 mi · 5–7 hrs · Classic "climb high sleep low." Ascend to Lava Tower 15,190' for lunch, then descend through giant Senecio forest into Barranco Valley. The massive Barranco Wall dominates tomorrow's view.
Jul 10
Day 5
Friday July 10
Barranco Wall scramble → Karanga Camp
⛺ Karanga Camp · 13,200' / 4,035m
3 mi · 4–5 hrs · Famous hands-and-feet Barranco Wall scramble — rewarded with panoramic views at the top. Then South Circuit path to Karanga. Rest under stars — tomorrow gets serious.
Jul 11
Day 6
Saturday July 11
Karanga → Kosovo Camp (summit eve)
⛺ Kosovo Camp · 16,000' ⭐ Kosovo Advantage
2.6 mi · 4–5 hrs · Steady climb past Barafu to Kosovo Camp on an exposed ridge. Kibo and Mawenzi both visible. Early dinner ~5 PM · Sleep 7 PM · Wake midnight. Kosovo Advantage: ClimbKili's exclusive high camp puts you 700' above other groups — shorter summit push, higher success rate.
Kosovo Advantage ⭐Summit Eve
Jul 11→12
SUMMIT
Saturday night / Sunday July 12
🏔️ SUMMIT NIGHT → Uhuru Peak 19,341' → Mweka Camp
⛺ Mweka Camp · 10,000' after descent
13 mi total · Ascent 6–8 hrs · Descent 4–6 hrs · Midnight departure in the cold and dark (-10°F wind chill). Stella Point at sunrise — one of the most emotional moments imaginable. Uhuru Peak 1 hour beyond. Stand at 19,341' — the Roof of Africa. Descend all the way to Mweka Camp for celebration dinner.
Uhuru Peak 19,341'Roof of Africa
Jul 13
Day 8
Monday July 13
Mweka Camp → Mweka Gate → Villa Maua, Arusha
🏨 Villa Maua · First night of luxury!
6 mi · 3–4 hrs · Final rainforest descent. ClimbKili vehicle meets you at Mweka Gate. Tip porters and guides here (~$250–300/person in USD cash). Transfer to Villa Maua for hot showers, incredible food and the satisfaction of having summited Africa's highest peak.

Transfer to Matemwe — the longest and widest beach on Zanzibar. Infinity Beach has a spa, pool and direct beach access. 15 min by boat to Mnemba Atoll. July is peak season — warm, dry, 30m+ underwater visibility. Settle in and decompress after 17 intense days.

🤿 Snorkel / Scuba — Mnemba Atoll
🏨 Infinity Beach Boutique Hotel & Spa
Mnemba Atoll is East Africa's most celebrated dive site — a protected marine reserve 15 min by boat. Both PADI certified → 2-tank dive day. Turtles, dolphins, reef sharks, moray eels, 500+ fish species, 30m+ visibility. Pre-book June with One Ocean Zanzibar, Fun Divers, or Spanish Dancer Divers.
